## Transformation journey
**Current state:** "High-functioning exhaustion." The "eldest daughter" archetype — hyper-competent, financially independent, perpetually in control. A cycle of over-performing, over-thinking, and "keeping it together" while the nervous system stays in chronic, low-grade contraction. Disconnected from their own body — it's a tool to manage, not an oracle to hear. Successful on paper, but a hollow ache for "more" — a desire to stop editing themselves and finally surrender.

**Desired state:** To move from "survival" to "aliveness." To drop the armor, trust instinctual intelligence, inhabit their body with ease. To experience deep, unedited pleasure and sexual vitality, and the freedom to be "seen" without performing. To reach "sovereign surrender" — powerful enough to let go, trust their own desires as a compass, and live a life that's truly, unapologetically theirs.

## Core problem (urgency gateway)
**Chronic nervous system "armouring"** and the inability to switch off "go-go-go" survival mode.

**Why it's urgent now:** Their old strategies — control, intellect, hard work — are stopping working, leading to burnout, physical tension, and a growing existential emptiness that now threatens their professional and personal success.

**Failed solutions they've already tried:**
- Traditional talk therapy — too intellectual, doesn't address the body
- Standard "hustle" productivity hacks
- Generic self-care routines that feel like another chore
- Over-analyzing their own psychology without somatic release
