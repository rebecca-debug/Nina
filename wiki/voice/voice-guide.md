The 2026-10-18 source does include one verbatim first-person passage from Nina describing her audience — see [audience/icp-primary.md](../audience/icp-primary.md) — which is a stronger voice signal than anything else ingested so far.

## Tone
Commanding, sophisticated, sharp, and deeply grounded.

## Signature phrases — pain
holding it all together, head on a stick, always in control, the weight of the world, numb to my own needs, performing my life

## Signature phrases — desire
fully alive, sovereign surrender, body as oracle, stop editing myself, visceral pleasure, wild intelligence

## Emotional triggers to write toward
aliveness, permission, sovereignty, instinct, magnetism, unapologetic

## Core slogan / line
"Reclaiming your body as the ultimate boardroom for your life."

## Banned / avoid
Not yet defined by Nina. Based on the research's explicit market gap, avoid:
- Clinical/medical framing ("disorder," "crisis" used diagnostically)
- Generic self-help "hacks" language
- Vague, guru-sounding copy (flagged explicitly in the AI brand system prompt pack as something to avoid)
- "Ethereal/soft" framing — the current positioning explicitly wants "glamorous/powerful" instead
