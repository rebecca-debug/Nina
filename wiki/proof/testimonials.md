## Consent note — read before using any of this externally
Source documents mix clients who explicitly granted name-use permission, clients
who explicitly asked to stay anonymous, and clients with no stated preference
either way. Where a document doesn't say, **do not assume permission to publish a
real name** — confirm with Nina first. Initials, first-name-only, or "Anonymous"
labels in the sources may reflect the client's own choice of how much to reveal,
not a lack of a full name on file.
